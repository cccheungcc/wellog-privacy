Wellog Support

Wellog is a daily health log for food, water, blood pressure, body weight and alcohol, with AI nutrition estimates from meal photos.

Contact

Email wellog@9s.hk. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened — that is usually enough to identify the cause without any further back and forth.

How do I log a meal

Open the Food tab and tap Take Photo or Choose Photo. An estimate appears in a few seconds. Check the numbers, adjust the portion if the amount is wrong, then tap Save in the bar at the bottom. Nothing is recorded until you tap Save.

The estimate looks wrong

Estimates come from a photo, so they are approximate by nature. Use the Portion section to set what the estimate covers and how much you actually had, and the share buttons if you only ate part of a dish. You can also open Fine-tune the numbers and type exact values. Whatever is on screen when you tap Save is what gets recorded.

Nothing is appearing in Apple Health

Apple Health access is optional and controlled by iOS, not by Wellog. Open the Settings app, go to Apps, then Health, then Data Access & Devices, find Wellog, and check that the categories you want are switched on. Records saved before you granted access are not written retroactively.

Water logged from the widget is missing from Apple Health

A Home Screen widget cannot ask for Health permission, so widget entries are written to Apple Health the next time you open the app. Open Wellog once and they will sync, keeping their original times.

Subscription terms

Wellog Premium is an auto-renewable monthly subscription that unlocks unlimited food photo scans. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase, and it renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

The subscription is covered by Apple's standard Terms of Use (EULA): https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

How do I cancel the subscription

Subscriptions are billed and managed by Apple, not by Wellog. Open the Settings app, tap your name at the top, tap Subscriptions, then choose Wellog. Cancelling there stops future renewals; the current period runs to its end.

I subscribed but the app still shows the free limit

Open the upgrade screen and tap Restore Purchases. If it still does not appear, check that the Apple Account signed in on the device is the one that bought the subscription.

How do I get my data out

Open Profile and use Export to CSV. You can also import CSV files exported from an earlier version of Wellog on the same screen.

How do I delete my data

Your logs are stored on your device, so deleting the app removes them. Data written to Apple Health belongs to Apple Health and is deleted there, in the Health app. Meal photos are sent only for nutrition analysis when you choose to scan and are not kept as your health record.
