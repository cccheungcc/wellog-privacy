Wellog Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 24, 2026

Wellog is a wellness logging app for blood pressure, weight, food, water, alcohol, activity, and habit coaching. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, medication instructions, or emergency medical advice.

Data You Enter

The app may store information you enter, including your name, age, sex, height, weight, activity level, health goal, diet notes, blood pressure readings, body weight records, food logs, water logs, alcohol logs, and notes.

These records are stored by the app on your device. When you allow Apple Health access, supported records may also be read from or written to Apple Health. The app backend is not used as your personal health record timeline.

Apple Health

If you choose to connect Apple Health, the app may request permission to read or write selected Apple Health categories, including blood pressure, heart rate, body weight, steps, active energy, dietary energy, nutrients, water, and alcoholic beverages.

Apple Health access is controlled by Apple's permission screen and iPhone Settings. You can change Apple Health permissions at any time in iPhone Settings.

HealthKit data is not used for advertising, marketing, or use-based data mining.

Food Analysis

When you choose to scan a food photo or estimate food from typed text, the app sends that photo or text to the Wellog backend for nutrition estimation. The backend sends the request to the configured AI model provider to estimate meal name, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, sodium, and related meal details.

Do not upload photos or notes that contain information you do not want analyzed for meal estimation.

Backend and Security

The backend is used for food analysis and app security checks. It should not store your full personal health timeline. API provider keys are kept on the backend and are not included in the iPhone app.

The backend may process technical information needed to operate and protect the service, such as request timing, error information, and security verification data. Logs should not contain food image bodies, API keys, Apple Health records, or full personal health timelines.

Export

The app includes CSV export so you can keep a backup or review your local app records outside the app.

Medical Safety

Coach guidance is for general wellness habits and tracking. It is not a substitute for professional medical advice. For severe blood pressure readings, chest pain, breathing trouble, fainting, stroke symptoms, or any urgent concern, seek medical help immediately. For medication, diagnosis, or treatment decisions, consult a qualified clinician.
